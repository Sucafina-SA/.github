## 🚀 About Us

Sucafina is a commodity merchanting company trading exclusively in coffee. We started in 1905 as a trading company dealing with 
sugar, coffee and finance, a legacy still visible in Sucafina’s name today: Sucre (SU), Café (CA) Finance (FINA).

Today, Sucafina is a leading, sustainable Farm to Roaster coffee company, with more than 1,500 employees in 44 countries. The 
group is one of the leading coffee trading houses in the world and one of the few focused entirely on coffee.
